# User Manual

What you can see and do depends on your role - if a button or page mentioned here isn't visible to
you, your role doesn't have that permission; ask your administrator rather than assuming it's broken.

## Logging in

Your username/password if you're an AD user is the same one you use for everything else at your
organization. If your organization isn't using AD, your administrator gives you a username/password
directly. Sessions stay active while you're using the app; if you're idle long enough you'll be asked
to sign in again next time you do something.

## Dashboard

Your landing page - active project count, your own pending approvals, physical files currently
issued/overdue, a stage-status breakdown, and your most recently updated projects. Everything here is
scoped to what you're allowed to see - it's not a global company-wide view unless your role is one
that can see everything.

## Finding things

- **Search bar** (top of every page) - type at least 2 characters, press Enter. Searches projects,
  documents, and physical files at once.
- **Projects** - the full project list, filterable by customer/status/search text.
- **Document Library** - every document you have access to, across all projects, not just one.

## Working with a project's document checklist

Open a project → its checklist is grouped by stage. Each row is a document requirement:

- **Upload** - click Upload next to a requirement, pick your file. If that requirement needs
  approval, the document goes to Under Review, not straight to Approved - check back or wait for a
  notification.
- A stage only shows **Completed** once every mandatory requirement on it has an approved document -
  uploading isn't the same as being done.
- If you think a requirement genuinely doesn't apply to this specific project, ask whoever has
  override permission (typically your Document Coordinator) rather than leaving it blank
  indefinitely - a documented "not applicable" with a reason is different from an ignored gap.

## Approving documents

If your role routes you approvals: **Document Approvals → Pending** shows what's waiting on you
specifically (not everything in the system - only steps actually routed to your role/department).
Approve or Reject with an optional comment. Rejecting doesn't delete anything - if there was a
previously-approved version, the document stays on that version until a new one is approved.

## Requesting access to a confidential document

If a document is above your normal access level, the download button is replaced with **Request
Access** - fill in why you need it, and it goes to whoever approves access requests for that
confidentiality tier. Once approved, you can download it (the approval doesn't expire immediately,
but isn't permanent either - ask your administrator about your organization's policy here).

## Physical files

If your project has physical files (original signed documents, drawings, etc. kept in a physical
archive): **Physical Files** page, or from the project itself. To borrow one: **Request** (with a
purpose and expected return date) → wait for approval if it's above your ceiling → once issued, it's
checked out to you and due back by the date shown. If you need more time, use **Request Extension**
before the due date rather than after - a rejected extension keeps the *original* due date, it never
silently extends anyway.

## Type Test Certificates

**Type Test Certificates** page - if your role has upload access, you can add or renew a certificate
here. Everyone with view access can browse the registry and download any certificate's file. The
color-coded tag tells you at a glance: green (valid), orange (expiring within a month), red
(expired).

## Notifications

The bell icon (top-right) shows your unread count and a list of recent notifications - click one to
mark it read, or "Mark all read." What you get notified about (and by email/WhatsApp/in-app) is set
by your administrator in Notification Rules, not something you configure per-user today.

## Reports

**Reports** page - if your role has report access, you can export Project Status, Document Register,
Physical File Register, or Overdue/Extension reports as CSV, Excel, or PDF. An export never shows you
anything you couldn't already see on-screen.
